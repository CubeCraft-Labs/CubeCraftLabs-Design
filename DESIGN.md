# CubeCraftLabs — Brand Spec

**v1 · the maker / R&D side**

---

## 01 · Positioning

CubeCraftLabs is the workshop, prototyping and R&D branch of CubeCraft Creations. Where Creations is the storefront of finished handmade goods (warm, rounded, gradient, Nunito), Labs is the bench where things get designed and built — **technical, dark, precise**. It shares the cube DNA and the CubeCraft name; everything else deliberately diverges to signal "this is where the making happens."

## 03 · Typography

- **Family** — JetBrains Mono (loaded from Google Fonts)
- **Weights** — 400 body · 500 label · 700 heading · 800 display
- **Wordmark** — weight 800, mixed-case, `0.02em` tracking, "Labs" in gold, optional cursor block
- **Caps labels & buttons** — uppercase, `0.08em` tracking
- **Corners** — square everywhere (`border-radius: 0`)

---

## 04 · Cube geometry

One isometric cube drives every mark.

- viewBox `0 0 200 200`
- top vertex `(100,44)`
- shoulders `(146,70)` / `(54,70)`
- waist `(100,96)`
- base `(100,150)`

Rendered as a gold wireframe (assembled) or exploded for the primary.

---

## 06 · Usage

### Do
- Keep the cube gold on a charcoal (`#1a1a1a`) ground.
- Set all type in JetBrains Mono, square corners.
- Reserve gold for the mark, "Labs", accents & active states.
- Use the exploded cube as the primary; assembled cube elsewhere.

### Don't
- Don't apply the Creations blue→purple gradient or Nunito.
- Don't round the corners or use a light background.
- Don't fill the "Labs" block solid gold (outline only).
- Don't recolor the cube or add faces beyond the three visible.
