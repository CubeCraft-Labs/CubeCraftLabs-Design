The CubeCraftLabs isometric cube mark as inline SVG — gold on charcoal, one geometry with three renderings.

```jsx
<CubeMark variant="exploded" size={64} />
```

Variants: `wireframe` (default, compact/inline), `exploded` (primary brand mark), `grid` (graph-paper badge stamp). Always render on a charcoal (#1a1a1a) surface. Keep the mark gold; don't recolor faces or add faces beyond the three visible.
